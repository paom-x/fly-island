## 更新内容

**停在侧边**
岛原来只能贴屏幕顶边，现在可以停到左边或右边：设置 → 通用 → 位置 → 停靠边。选侧边时窗口竖起来，岛转四分之一圈贴着那条边，收起时朝同一条边缩回去，鼠标命中、展开与点击穿透都跟着转。防烧屏的那一点点位移也改成沿竖边上下走。

## What's new

**Docked on the side**
The island used to sit only on the top edge; now it can sit on the left or right one: Settings -> General -> Position -> Docked edge. On a side the window stands on its end, the island turns a quarter turn against that edge, and tucking slides it back into the same one; the cursor hit test, the expansion and click-through turn with it. The burn-in step follows the edge too, running up and down instead of across.
