## 更新内容

- **固定在顶部**：去掉了上一个版本加的「停靠边」。岛重新固定在屏幕顶边，设置里不再出现顶边、左边、右边的选项；之前选过侧边的，升级后自动回到顶部，收起、贴边、防烧屏都与原来一样。
- **机器占用页**：展开岛顶栏新增「机器占用」页，勾选读数后出现，显示处理器、内存、显卡、温度和网络的走势；每 2 秒采样一次，保留最近两分钟，离开页面即停。

## What's new

- **Back at the top**: the docked-edge option from the previous release is gone. The island sits at the top edge again, and Settings no longer offers top, left or right; anyone who picked a side returns to the top after updating, with tucking and the burn-in guard unchanged.
- **Machine load page**: the expanded island now has a Machine load page. When any reading is enabled, it shows recent trends for processor, memory, graphics, temperature and network; it samples every 2 seconds, keeps the last two minutes, and stops as soon as you leave the page.
