## 更新内容

- **固定在顶部**：去掉了上一个版本加的「停靠边」。岛重新固定在屏幕顶边，设置里不再出现顶边、左边、右边的选项；之前选过侧边的，升级后自动回到顶部，收起、贴边、防烧屏都与原来一样。

## What's new

- **Back at the top**: the docked-edge option from the previous release is gone. The island sits at the top edge again, and Settings no longer offers top, left or right; anyone who picked a side returns to the top after updating, with tucking and the burn-in guard unchanged.
